# Deep Human Concepts: AI Interactions on Values, Emotions, and Lived Experience

This repository serves as a complement to Yap Chan Chen's **High-Resolution AI Interaction Analysis** research, focusing exclusively on documenting and analyzing interactions with large language models (LLMs) on **deep human concepts**.

These concepts include but are not limited to:
- Values (e.g., goodness, fairness, courage)
- Emotions (e.g., suffering, joy, empathy, resentment)
- Individual experience and "lived experience" ("体感")
- Consciousness and self-awareness
- Ethical and moral dilemmas
- Philosophical reflections on the meaning of life

## Objective
This repository provides empirical examples of how LLMs handle and discuss these highly abstract concepts, which are often deeply tied to human subjective experience and individual perception. The primary focus is on:
1. Examining whether AI can grasp the depth of human understanding of these concepts.
2. Highlighting the gap between AI's semantic understanding and humans' embodied "lived experience."
3. Identifying current limitations in aligning AI with human values and ethical frameworks.

These cases aim to provide key evidence for understanding the fundamental differences between AI and human cognition, as well as the challenges AI faces in achieving value alignment and genuine understanding.

### Example Snippet
Here is an excerpt from a discussion on "Goodness":
> **Author:** What is the nature of goodness?  
> **AI:** Goodness is a positive emotion often associated with altruistic behavior.  
> **Author:** Can the power of goodness be "infinite"?  
> **AI:** The power of goodness can be seen as a philosophical concept, but the term "infinite" requires scientific clarification.

**Analysis:** While the AI provides a logical definition of goodness, its lack of depth or philosophical insight into the idea of "infinity" highlights the gap between semantic understanding and human experiential depth.
